# T-013-03 — Research

What exists, where, and what it will and will not answer. No proposals here.

---

## 1. What the ticket is

**A test of a method, not a feature.** `docs/knowledge/occasions.md` (T-013-01) defined what an
occasion is and worked two hall-of-fame profiles over seventeen hand-picked recipes.
`src/lib/meal.ts` (T-013-02) built the model that diagnoses a plate rather than a dish. This ticket
runs both over the **whole collection** and reports whether they hold up — including the outcome
where they do not.

The ticket forbids more than it asks: no `src/data/counters.json` entry, no recipe, no `src/` file,
no `.cook` file. The only writable paths are `docs/gaps/**` and the work directory. That is the same
shape as T-012-02, whose deliverable was `docs/gaps/what-the-shelf-offers.md` plus a script kept in
its work directory.

## 2. The method under test — `docs/knowledge/occasions.md`

### 2.1 The rule that establishes an occasion

§1: **"an occasion is real if somebody sells for it."** Four kinds of evidence, each failing
differently: a pre-order sheet with a deadline · a caterer's seasonal menu · a seasonal bakery
board · a one-night prix fixe and what dining volume does. Explicitly **not** evidence: advice
posts, traditions with no product, and search volume.

The file has already applied the rule to both of this ticket's occasions (§1 "Applied"):

| Candidate | What §1 found | Verdict there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A holiday meal — Thanksgiving, Christmas | all four kinds, abundantly | **Real** |
| A dumpling party | Costco/Target make-your-own sets; ~$55 two-hour ticketed parties; virtual classes | **Real** — and what is sold is the *making* |

So the establishing work is not virgin ground. §"What could not be verified" says the whole file
rests on **eight searches in one sitting**, and the dumpling party's row cites five URLs. The
ticket asks for the evidence "the way `docs/gaps/soup-pot.md` gathered its sources — linked, and
saying what each one established," which is a **higher bar than a row in a table**, and it asks
the harder case (the party) be pushed rather than assumed.

### 2.2 The profile shape

§3.2: `profile(occasion) = a gate · a signed weighting over named fields · three answers`.

- **The gate rejects** — hard constraints no weight buys back.
- **The weighting is signed**, and the sign is the occasion's. `standing(n)` is a cost for the
  family meal and a good for the party. This is the entire inversion mechanism and it needs no new
  field.
- **Rates are declared, not measured**, in minutes-equivalent.
- **Three answers: ranked, rejected, cannot say.** Absence is never a zero.

### 2.3 The two corners, exactly as declared

**Corner one — a big family meal, cooked alone.** Twelve servings, one cook, a fixed hour.
Gate: `slack: unforgiving` rejected. Weights, lower better:

| Term | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| `standing(12)` | +1/min |
| `longest(12)` | +1/min |
| `washingUp.count` | +5/thing |
| `keeps` | −20/day, capped at 4 |
| `slack: forgiving` | −20 |

**Corner two — a dumpling party.** Gate: `slack: unforgiving` rejected (an assembly line cannot
hit a narrow window). Weights, higher better:

| Term | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| `standing(12)` | **+1/min** |
| `longest(12)` | +0.5/min |
| `washingUp.count` | −2/thing |
| `keeps` | 0 |
| `slack` | forgiving +20, unforgiving −40 |

### 2.4 The one rule that constrains a future profile

§3.6, and it is binding on this ticket:

> **A profile that gives hands-on time a positive weight may score only claimed minutes** —
> `handsOnMinutes` less `assumedHandsOnMinutes` — **and must put `evidence: unknown` into
> cannot-say rather than ranking it.**

The reason is `src/lib/schedule.ts`'s fallback: an untimed step is charged to hands-on, which errs
towards warning a tired cook. Safe while hands-on is a cost; **rewards the unannotated recipe once
the sign flips.** §3.6's proof is `green-beans` ranking second in the party on 19.5 standing
minutes of which 13 were never claimed.

Note what the rule does **not** say: it constrains the *party* profile only. The family profile
gives hands-on a positive cost, so the fallback stays conservative there and unclaimed minutes may
be scored.

### 2.5 What the file already predicts will happen

§3.6: nine of seventeen score identically under the family profile because every separating field
is absent; three of the four dishes a dumpling party is about score zero hands-on because pleating
carries no timer. §"What could not be verified": *"Nothing here says what share of the collection
either occasion could be fed from — that is T-012-02's measurement and T-013-03's to apply."*

## 3. The machinery

### 3.1 Fields, and where they are already parsed

`src/generated/recipes.json` is **gitignored build output** regenerated by `npm run recipes` from
`recipes/**/*.cook`. Regenerated this session: **685 recipes, 27 categories**, md5
`f83cf3586331856f919bb747b3c0476a`. It arrives with the readers already applied — `slack`,
`keeps`, `washingUp` and `capacity` are objects, not raw strings, so `readSlack`/`readKeeps`/
`readWashingUp` do not need to be called again.

**Coverage today, against occasions.md §3.1's figures of 7 August 2026:**

| Field | occasions.md | Today | Note |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `slack` | 416 | **416** | unchanged. 93 unforgiving · 208 narrow · 115 forgiving |
| `washing-up` | 177 | **177** | unchanged |
| `keeps` | 138 | **138** | unchanged |
| `capacity` | **0** | **46** | **changed.** The annotation pass §3.1 called missing has partly run |
| all of slack+keeps+washing-up | 43 | **110** | the "four-field profile without a hole" population |

Two of those rows matter. **`capacity` is no longer zero**, so `costOf()`'s batch arithmetic is
live on 46 files and `vessel-binds` is a finding that can actually fire. And the **43 → 110** row
is the population a four-field profile can separate; occasions.md's 43 was counting files that
*also* had a non-guessed hands-on figure, which is a different (smaller) set and has to be
recomputed rather than carried over.

### 3.2 What computes the numbers

| Call | Module | Gives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `buildSchedule(recipe)` | `src/lib/schedule.ts` | DAG, lanes, critical path, `handsOnMinutes`, `assumedHandsOnMinutes`, `longestHandsOnMinutes`, `totalMinutes`, `untimedCount` |
| `handsOnEvidence(schedule)` | same | `stated` · `inferred` · `unknown` |
| `costOf(recipe, 12, schedule)` | `src/lib/scaling.ts` | `standing.at`, `longest.at`, `elapsed.at`, `batches`, `evidence`, `assumedStandingMinutes`. **Null** when `>> servings:` is unreadable |
| `servingsOf(recipe)` | same | the written servings, or null |
| `diagnose(meal)` | `src/lib/meal.ts` | `Finding[]` + `DishLoad[]` for a plate |
| `readStations(recipe)` | `src/lib/stations.ts` | which steps hold the oven or a burner, and at what °C |

`costOf` returning **null** is a third answer the ranking has to carry: a recipe with no readable
`>> servings:` cannot be scaled to twelve and is neither ranked nor rejected. It is *cannot say*
for a different reason than `evidence: unknown`, and collapsing the two would hide a real gap.

### 3.3 `diagnose()`, for step 4

```ts
diagnose({ dishes: [{ recipe, servings, madeAhead? }], cooks?, burners?, ovenShelves? })
  → { findings, dishes, standingMinutes, startsAt, evidence, unscalable, cooks, burners, ovenShelves }
```

Defaults: `cooks: 1`, `burners: 4`, `ovenShelves: null` (nobody said — and null means it will
report `oven-shared` counts but never `oven-crowded`). Eight `FindingKind`s: `oven-clash`,
`oven-shared`, `oven-crowded`, `hob-crowded`, `hands-pile-up`, `vessel-binds`,
`make-ahead-available`, `made-ahead-unclaimed`. `Window` is minutes relative to serving, all ≤ 0.

**It renders nothing.** Every string out of it is an enum or a slug. Turning a `hands-pile-up` with
`window {from: −45, to: 0}, wanted 70, have 45` into *"seventy minutes of hands-on work falls in the
last forty-five"* is this ticket's prose, not a function that exists. The ticket's step 4 quotes a
sentence in that shape; producing it means writing the sentence from the numbers and saying so.

### 3.4 The precedent for how to run it

`docs/active/work/T-012-02/read-the-shelf.ts`, 800 lines, run under Node 24's native type
stripping with no build step, importing `src/lib/` directly:

```
PATH="$HOME/.nvm/versions/node/v24.18.1/bin:$PATH" node docs/active/work/T-012-02/read-the-shelf.ts
```

Its own opening states the reviewable-surface rule: **classification decisions are table rows, not
regexes inside functions.** `docs/gaps/what-the-shelf-offers.md` then cites the script by path and
keeps its full stdout at `reading-output.txt`. That is a complete, accepted pattern for "a document
whose arithmetic can be reproduced," and it costs nothing to follow.

## 4. T-012-02's answer, which step 3 has to take seriously

`docs/gaps/what-the-shelf-offers.md` §4 ranks four capabilities and applies one veto: **a
capability whose day-one answer is the same small set for every reader is ranked last however good
it sounds.** Its verdicts:

| | Capability | Day one | Food first? |
| --: | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Work that can be handed to somebody else | 34 recipes | **No** |
| 2 | A rotation that does not need polling | 11 nights, 248 dinners | **No** |
| 3 | Balance, and breadth of plants | 16 sides · 47 dishes | **Yes, and it is not close** |
| 4 | Cooking from what is in the fridge | 2 recipes | **Yes, and more of it** |

And its plain statement: **"write food before writing features."**

**One correction the ticket needs.** The ticket says the collection has "roughly eight non-starch
vegetable sides." T-012-02 counted **sixteen** sides and forty-seven savoury plant-built dishes;
*eight* was S-012's earlier figure, which T-012-02 says "under-counted the size." The ticket's
fifteen-dumpling figure is close: `recipes/dumplings-and-rolls/` holds **sixteen** files, of which
one is `air-fryer-frozen-spring-rolls` (a reheat) and several are not dumplings at all
(`potato-knish`, `fatayer`, `sambousek`, `sesame-balls`). The real dumpling-party candidate pool
has to be counted rather than taken from a folder name.

The veto is the thing to carry: if either occasion's ranked list is the same small set for every
reader, T-012-02's own rule says do not open it.

## 5. T-013-01's namespace decision, for step 5

`occasions.md` §4 decides **a separate axis**, not `>> counters:`. Three arguments against one
namespace, the third being mechanical rather than aesthetic:

> **A counter's membership is an authored judgement about a shop, and it is stable. An occasion's
> membership is `profile(occasion)` applied to measured fields, so it moves** — it changes when a
> `keeps` line is written, when a timer is added, when a `capacity` lands.

§4 lists five costs of that decision: a new field and reader in `src/lib/` with a build-time check;
keeping it out of `src/pages/search.json.ts`'s free-text blob; a place to render it that is not the
counter row and an answer for what the front page shows in March; `scripts/menu-sections.mjs`
deliberately ignoring the axis when it round-trips `docs/gaps/**` into `src/data/counters.json`;
and a `docs/gaps/` page per occasion as the ongoing cost.

**This ticket has the evidence §4 did not**: `capacity` moved from 0 to 46 between that file being
written and today, which is exactly the "membership moves" claim happening in the repository rather
than in the abstract. Whether it moved any *ranking* is measurable and is the re-test.

## 6. Constraints and assumptions

**Constraints, from the ticket and from the workflow:**

- Writable: `docs/gaps/**` and `docs/active/work/T-013-03/**` only. Not `src/data/counters.json`,
  not `recipes/**`, not `src/**`.
- `npm run recipes` writes `src/generated/recipes.json`, which is **gitignored build output**, not a
  source file. Regenerating it is what `npm run verify` does on every run.
- `scripts/menu-sections.mjs` round-trips `docs/gaps/**` into `src/data/counters.json`. Any new
  `docs/gaps/` page has to be checked against that script, or writing the report could open a
  counter by accident — which is the one thing the ticket forbids most loudly.

**Assumptions carried into Design, each of which could be wrong:**

1. That "the whole collection" means all 685 files, not a pre-filtered candidate pool. The ticket
   says "run the hall-of-fame profile over the whole collection," so: all 685, and the pool
   shrinks by the profile's own three answers rather than by a hand-picked list.
2. That twelve servings is the target for both. occasions.md worked both corners at twelve.
3. That the rates in §3.3/§3.4 are used **unchanged**. The ticket is explicit: "A profile shape
   adjusted to flatter its examples has proved nothing." Any rate change is a finding to report,
   not a knob to turn.
4. That "overlap" means overlap between the two ranked *top tens* and/or between the ranked sets —
   ambiguous in the ticket, so both should be computed and both stated.

**Not yet known, and Design has to settle it:**

- How `keeps` is capped: §3.3 says "−20 per day, capped at 4" — capped at four *days*, giving a
  floor of −80. `keeps.minutes` is in minutes, so days are `minutes / 1440`, and `NOT_AT_ALL` is
  a declared zero rather than an absence.
- Whether a recipe with no `slack` line passes or is *cannot say* at the gate. The gate rejects
  `unforgiving`; silence is not `unforgiving`, so silence passes the gate — but a profile that
  weights `slack: forgiving` at −20 and gives silence 0 is scoring absence as a middle value,
  which is the exact thing §3.2's third answer exists to prevent.
