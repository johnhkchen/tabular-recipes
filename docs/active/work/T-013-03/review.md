## 4. Open concerns, ranked

### 1. The party profile is confounded with written servings, and the report says so but does not fix it

**This is the biggest thing a reviewer should look at.** At a fixed target of twelve servings,
`costOf()` scales hands-on work by `wanted / written`, so the party profile — whose only large
positive term is `standing(12)` — partly ranks recipes by **how small a batch the file was written
for**. Seven of its top ten are written for four servings or fewer; two are written for one.

The report measures the effect (§2.3), names the fix as a design decision (§7.2 item 4), and states
in its closing that **it does not know how much of ρ = −0.591 survives a per-serving rate.** That
is honest and it is also a real limit on the headline number. It was not fixed here because fixing
it means changing `occasions.md`'s declared rates, and the ticket forbids exactly that: *"A profile
shape adjusted to flatter its examples has proved nothing."*

### 2. A data defect found and deliberately not fixed

`recipes/dressings-and-dips/birista.cook` declares `>> servings: 1 1/2 cups`. `servingsOf()` takes
the leading number and returns **1**, so a cup and a half of fried shallots is scaled by twelve and
becomes the **#2 dumpling-party dish in the entire collection**. One line, and the ticket forbids
touching `.cook` files. Reported in §2.3 and §7.2 item 3 for whoever owns the file.

### 3. `occasions.md` has three things wrong with it, and this ticket did not edit it

Reported in the report's §9.2:

1. §3.1 says **0 capacities declared**; it is now **46**.
2. §3.5's combined seventeen-row table **ranks four recipes its own gates reject**
   (`smoked-turkey-breast`, `turkey-brine`, `siu-mai`, `xiao-long-bao`).
3. §3.6's claimed-minutes rule constrains `standing` and says nothing about **`longest`**, which the
   party profile also weights positively and which has no assumed-minutes counterpart to subtract.
   A fallback minute barred from one term still pays into the other.

The file was not edited because it belongs to whoever maintains it and its own closing invites the
correction. **A reviewer who disagrees should say so** — this is the one judgement call in the
ticket that could reasonably have gone the other way, and `docs/knowledge/**` is inside the ticket's
writable set only by omission, not by permission.

### 4. One departure from `occasions.md` §3.2's spirit, recorded rather than hidden

A recipe with no `>> slack:` line is not `unforgiving`, so it passes the gate — but it also earns no
`forgiving` bonus, which is **scoring absence as a middle value**, the exact thing §3.2's third
answer exists to prevent. It is kept because the alternative — *cannot say* for all 269 files with
no `slack` line — would empty the family list and turn the exercise into a report about `slack`
coverage. `design.md` §2.1 records the departure; the report measures it (45% of the holiday
ranking has no `slack` line).

### 5. A wrong absence was published in a draft and corrected

The first version of the plate-vocabulary search read slugs and tags only and reported **"Stuffing.
Zero files."** `cornbread-dressing` is the collection's stuffing and declares it only in `aka`. The
script now reads slug, title, `aka` and tags; the candidate pool went 49 → 91 and the headline
recommendation went from six missing recipes to **five**. Called out in the report's own §5.1 and
in its closing, because reporting an absence that is not real is the failure mode the rest of the
document is about. **Every other absence claimed in the report should be read as *nothing matched
this vocabulary*, not as *this does not exist*.**
