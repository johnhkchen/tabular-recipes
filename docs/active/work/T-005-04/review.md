### The spot-check was random, and it was drawn twice

The twenty quoted in `progress.md` were selected by sorting all 373 paths on the MD5 of the path
and taking the first twenty — reproducible from `spotcheck-20.txt`, and not chosen. All twenty
name a specific failure and the moment it happens.

A second, whole-collection sweep looked for the opposite: any line that had reduced to a
restatement of its level. It flagged 49 candidates; reading them, **48 were false negatives of
the failure-verb regex** (it did not know *bulge*, *acrid*, *slips off*, *lacquers*, *khaki*).
**One was real** — `sauerkraut`, whose original slack line named no failure either. That is the
finding below.

### `sauerkraut` — a fact was rescued rather than cut

Its original line said only *"three weeks is when to start tasting … the white film skims off."*
The failure is in the file, inside step 3's paragraph:

> Nothing may sit above the brine: what is submerged ferments and what is exposed goes mouldy.

Step 3 carries a `>> step.3:` label, so **that paragraph renders nowhere** — it is part of
S-005's 278,833 unread characters. The line now carries it, at 96 characters. This is the only
place in the ticket where the collection gained a fact rather than losing one, and it is
`voice.md`'s rule applied in the direction it was written for.

## How the work was done, and why that matters for review

Two files carry it, both published with the artifacts:

- **`slack-after.tsv`** — 373 hand-authored rows: path, level, new reason. **This is the thing
  to review.** It is 373 lines of prose, diffable, without opening a single `.cook` file.
- **`apply-slack.mjs`** — validates every row before writing any file, then replaces exactly one
  line per file. It aborts on: a level disagreeing with the file, a reason over 200 characters,
  under 5 words, a duplicate path, a path outside `recipes/`, a file with zero or two slack
  lines, or a reason that would not read back through `readSlack` to itself.

It was proved before it was trusted — seven cases, five of them expected to abort, one round
trip, one idempotence check, and `git checkout` afterwards to leave no trace. The judgement is
human; the edit is mechanical. That split is why *"only the `>> slack:` line changed"* is a
guarantee rather than a claim: the script has no code path that can write anything else.

The rejected alternative was mechanical clause-1 truncation. It measures well (mean 107.9, only
4 over cap) and it is wrong: on **64 of 117 `forgiving` lines the first clause names no failure**
— it is *"three hours or four is the same beef"*. `design.md` §2 has the numbers.

## Test coverage

**No test was added, and that is a decision.** The cap is enforced by
`scripts/check-recipes.mjs`, which `npm run verify` runs first, against the same generated JSON a
vitest assertion would read. A second copy of `reason.length <= 200` in `slack.test.ts` would
restate the checker and drift from it — and T-005-07's job is to make that checker fail the build
on exactly this number.

### Gaps, stated plainly

- **Nothing automated can tell a named failure from a fluent restatement of a level.** The
  5-word floor is the only guard and it is not close to binding. This is answered with the random
  twenty and the whole-collection sweep, both quoted — evidence a human can check, not a test.
- **Nothing automated knows a safety fact was load-bearing.** Answered by the table of 36 in
  `progress.md`, quoted as after-text so a reviewer verifies by reading rather than by trusting.
- **`dispositions.mjs` uses a word-overlap heuristic** (a clause survives if half its content
  words persist). It over-reports when a clause was reworded heavily — several `+SAFETY-REVIEW`
  flags turned out to be rewordings that did survive. It is a net that catches too much, which is
  the correct direction for this job, but it is not proof of a drop.
- **Sibling recipes were given different sentences, not verified to be different facts.**
  `shio-tare` and `shoyu-tare` genuinely share one failure (boiled kombu goes bitter); they are
  phrased differently and both are true, but a reviewer may prefer one of them to carry a
  different fact.

## Open concerns

### 1. Nine `forgiving` recipes now read as though they have no give — recorded, not fixed

`do-chua`, `corned-beef` ×2, `braised-short-ribs-slow-cooker`, `chashu`, `lo-mai-gai`,
`rice-pudding`, `potato-knish`, `smoked-pork-ribs`. Each now says *has no room* / *no give* /
*not optional* / *cannot be cut* under a `forgiving` label, because the half that made the line
read as forgiving was the half that named nothing going wrong.

The recipes are not mis-rated so much as **two-legged**: the dish overall is patient and one step
in it is not, and the field has room for one fact. The Hazards section forbids re-rating and says
this is a finding, so it is one. Listed in full in `progress.md`. **If a reviewer wants these
moved to `narrow`, that is a one-word change per file and it belongs to a person, not this
ticket.**

### 2. Relocation was not available inside this ticket's boundary

The ticket asks that a load-bearing dropped warning be *"moved"* to an ingredient note or a step
label. Its own Scope section forbids editing anything but the `>> slack:` line, and an ingredient
note lives inside a step body — which T-005-06 owns and will rewrite from a list that would not
contain a late insertion. **Scope won**, and it turned out to cost nothing: the four dropped
clauses that carried a fact were each already present in the operation cell that renders
(`corned-beef`'s five-day brine, `nixtamalised-masa`'s eight-hour steep, `shoyu-tare`'s alcohol
boil-off). Nothing needed moving. Recorded because the next ticket in the chain may not be so
lucky.

### 3. 78 lines are still over `voice.md`'s aim of about 120

All are under the 200 cap, and the aim is explicitly an aim (`voice.md:118`, *"the caps are the
ceiling, not the aim"*). The distribution is now `p50 111 · p90 126 · p99 141 · max 151`, so the
tail is thin and shallow rather than a second population. Pushing every line under 120 would mean
dropping a second fact from lines that legitimately carry two — `cha-lua`'s 50°F **and** 165°F,
`chopped-liver`'s two-day life **and** the six-minute broil. **Those are worth 130 characters.**
