## Open concerns

### 1. Ingredient notes have no owning ticket — this is what blocks T-005-07

17 notes in 13 files are over the 80-character cap. S-005 assigns `slack:` to T-005-04, prose
rows to T-005-05 and step bodies to T-005-06, and protects operation cells by name. **Nothing
in the story owns ingredient notes.** So when T-005-07 flips line 67, the build fails on 13
files that no ticket was scheduled to clean.

The alternative was pinning the cap at the observed maximum of 172 so the number could never
fire. That buys a clean flip by recording that the field is fine when it is not, and the ticket
names that case ("a cap only the 757-char outlier fails is decoration"). The flag was taken
instead.

**The fix is small and it is one shelf.** 12 of the 13 files are the Cantonese soup shelf, where
the note carries a romanisation, a Chinese term and a paragraph about what the ingredient does:

> `naam bak hang; the sweet and the bitter kind together, about three to one, and the pairing
> is the point — the bitter kind goes in small and always cooked through, never raw` (172)

The 13th is `recipes/stews-and-braises/buri-daikon.cook` (84). Recommendation: fold them into
T-005-06, which is already editing the step bodies these notes sit inside — that is one extra
shelf on a ticket that touches 329 files. They are listed by name in `report.txt`.

Deciding that is a person's call, not this ticket's, which is why this is a note rather than a
block: the ticket's own acceptance criteria are all met, and nothing here is broken today.

### 2. The ticket's field table describes the discard too narrowly

Recorded because it changed a number this ticket published, and because T-005-06 works from
that number.

The ticket's table says the step-body discard is a property of steps that become operations.
`src/lib/tree.ts:129` applies `step.labelOverride ?? cleanLabel(step.rawLabel)` on **both**
sides of the `isOpStep` branch, so a step with no ingredients and a `>> step.N:` line also
renders the override and discards its own paragraph. That is 140 further steps in 109 files and
28,451 more unread characters.

The ticket's own worked example is the case: `tonkotsu-broth-instant-pot.cook` step 1 has no
ingredients, its 132-character `step.1:` renders as the header row, and the 472-character
paragraph beneath it goes nowhere.

The field is therefore measured as "any step carrying a `>> step.N:` line" — 2782 steps in 637
files, 278,833 characters. The 150 cap was re-derived on the wider field and held; it is now
the exact crossing point rather than near one. **T-005-06's list is 656 entries in 329 files,
not the ~550 in ~266 an earlier reading of the ticket would have produced.** Nothing is lost by
the correction; the scope is simply larger and correctly stated.

### 3. Two counting conventions are in circulation, and the caps use one

The story's "333 of 397 slack reasons over 200" and the ticket's "shortest run around 103, the
longest 306" are measurements of the whole `>> slack:` value, level word included. The rendered
payload is the reason alone — `Timeline.astro:315` prints `<b>{word}</b> — {reason}` — and the
cap governs that. On the reason, the same field measures 92 to 290, and 304 are over 200.

A writer counting their own line should subtract the level and the dash: a 200-character reason
is about 211 characters as written. `voice.md` and the report both speak in rendered
characters, consistently.
